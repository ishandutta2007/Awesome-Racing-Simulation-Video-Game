**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Physics-Based Racing, Driving Simulation & Open-Source Racing Engines*  

**Last updated: October 2026**



This repository tracks notable **commercial racing simulations** and **open-source projects** that deliver realistic vehicle dynamics, track racing, and driving experiences — from casual arcade racers to hardcore physics simulators used by professional drivers.



**Examples** include Forza Motorsport, Gran Turismo 7, Assetto Corsa Competizione, iRacing, Automobilista 2, rFactor 2, Project CARS 2, F1 23, RaceRoom Racing Experience, and Dirt Rally 2.0 (the category leaders).

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[iRacing](https://www.iracing.com/)**  

  **The gold standard for competitive online racing simulation** — laser-scanned tracks, precise physics, and the world's most developed sim racing esports scene. **Subscription-based** ($13/month) with additional content purchases. **The reference for online racing** — used by professional drivers and teams.



- **[Assetto Corsa Competizione](https://www.assettocorsa.net/competizione/)**  

  **The definitive GT3/GT4 racing simulator** — officially licensed by SRO Motorsports Group. Unreal Engine 4 visuals with best-in-class force feedback and physics. **The standard for GT racing simulation** — used by real GT drivers for training.



- **[Forza Motorsport](https://forzamotorsport.net/)**  

  **The most accessible racing sim** — beautiful visuals, extensive car roster, and Forza Motorsport 2023's improved physics. **The best entry point for console racers** — available on Xbox and PC Game Pass.



- **[Gran Turismo 7](https://www.gran-turismo.com/)**  

  **The legendary PlayStation racing franchise** — unmatched car culture, stunning visuals, and deep tuning. **The best racing sim for PlayStation** — used by real drivers for training.



- **[Automobilista 2](https://www.automobilista2.com/)**  

  **Brazilian racing simulator with the MADNESS engine** — exceptional physics, dynamic weather, and diverse content. **The best sim for South American racing** — from karts to F1.



- **[rFactor 2](https://www.studio-397.com/)**  

  **The most moddable racing simulator** — used by professional teams for driver-in-the-loop training. **The engine behind many professional simulators** — physics-focused with extensive mod support.



- **[Project CARS 2](https://www.projectcarsgame.com/)**  

  **The most complete racing sim package** — from karts to IndyCar, with dynamic weather and time. **The best all-in-one racing simulator** — though online servers are largely shut down.



- **[F1 23](https://www.ea.com/games/f1/f1-23)**  

  **The official F1 game** — realistic handling, story mode (Braking Point), and all official tracks/cars. **The best F1 experience** for fans.



- **[RaceRoom Racing Experience](https://www.raceroom.com/)**  

  **Free-to-play racing sim with premium content** — exceptional sound design and physics. **The best free entry to sim racing** — optional paid content.



- **[Dirt Rally 2.0](https://www.dirtgame.com/)**  

  **The definitive rally simulation** — brutal difficulty, realistic stages, and superb force feedback. **The standard for rally sim racing** .



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Speed Dreams](https://github.com/speed-dreams/speed-dreams)**  

  **The leading open-source racing simulator**, GPL-2.0 licensed . **Fork of TORCS with modern graphics, improved physics, and active development** — 100+ cars, 100+ tracks . **Cross-platform** (Windows, macOS, Linux) . **The most complete open-source racing sim** — 20+ years of lineage from TORCS . **Best for players wanting a full open-source racing experience** .



- **[Stunt Rally](https://github.com/stuntrally/stuntrally)**  

  **Open-source rally and stunt driving game**, GPL-3.0 licensed . **3D tracks with loops, jumps, and obstacles** — physics-based driving . **The most fun open-source racing game** — arcade-style with simulation elements . **Best for players wanting rally and stunt gameplay** .



- **[VDrift](https://github.com/vdrift/vdrift)**  

  **Open-source drift racing simulator**, GPL-2.0 licensed . **Focus on drift physics and realistic car handling** . **The best open-source drift-specific sim** . **Best for drift enthusiasts** .



- **[Trigger Rally](https://github.com/TriggerRally/server)**  

  **Open-source rally game in the browser**, MIT licensed . **Lightweight and accessible** — runs in any modern browser . **The easiest entry to open-source racing** — no installation required . **Best for quick browser-based rally fun** .



- **[TORCS](https://github.com/fmirus/torcs-1.3.7)**  

  **The Open Racing Car Simulator — the original open-source racing sim**, GPL-2.0 licensed . **The foundation for Speed Dreams** — historically significant . **Mature but dated** — best for understanding racing sim architecture . **Best for historical interest and engine study** .



- **[OpenRCT2](https://github.com/OpenRCT2/OpenRCT2)**  

  **Open-source reimplementation of RollerCoaster Tycoon 2**, GPL-3.0 licensed with **15,000+ GitHub stars** . **Not a racing sim** — but included for its track design and management gameplay . **Best for theme park racing fans** .



- **[SuperTuxKart](https://github.com/supertuxkart/stk-code)**  

  **Open-source kart racing game**, GPL-3.0 licensed with **4,000+ GitHub stars** . **Mario Kart-style racing** — 20+ tracks, 20+ characters, and multiplayer . **The most complete open-source kart racer** . **Best for family-friendly open-source racing** .



- **[Kart Racing Game (various)](https://github.com/topics/kart-racing-game)**  

  Various open-source kart racers including **Karting Superstars** and **NoGravity** — **best for casual racing** .



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **OpenTTD** — Transport simulation with racing elements (not pure racing) .

- **Rigs of Rods** — Vehicle simulation sandbox with racing potential, GPL-3.0 licensed .

- **BeamNG.tech** — Soft-body physics simulation (not open-source but academically available) .

- **FlightGear** — Open-source flight simulator (not racing, but vehicle simulation) .

- **Xtreme Rally** — Open-source rally game .

- **OpenRacing** — Community project for open-source racing .

- **RaceGame** — Open-source racing game frameworks .



**Frameworks for building custom racing simulations**: Choose based on scope. **Speed Dreams** for a complete open-source racing sim with modern graphics . **Stunt Rally** for rally and stunt driving . **VDrift** for drift physics . **Trigger Rally** for lightweight browser racing . **TORCS** for understanding racing sim architecture . **SuperTuxKart** for kart racing . Note that true professional racing simulation with laser-scanned tracks, OEM-licensed vehicles, and force-feedback fidelity remains primarily commercial territory; open-source projects provide strong physics, track, and vehicle foundations that require community building for complete racing experiences.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Racing simulations often support mods and custom content. **Verify mod sources** — malicious mods can compromise systems. Use official repositories and community-vetted sources.

- **Open-source racing sims vary in polish** — Speed Dreams is complete but dated graphically; Stunt Rally is fun but arcade-focused; VDrift is niche . Evaluate against your expectations.

- **Force feedback support varies** — commercial sims (iRacing, ACC, rFactor 2) have superior force feedback implementation. Open-source sims may not support all wheels or provide the same fidelity .

- **Some open-source racing games require original game assets** — OpenRCT2 requires RollerCoaster Tycoon 2 files. It is an engine reimplementation, not a full game distribution .

- The open-source ecosystem provides strong physics, track, and vehicle foundations, but **laser-scanned tracks, OEM-licensed vehicles, and professional force-feedback fidelity** remain primarily commercial offerings.
